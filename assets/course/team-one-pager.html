The problem

01

AI builds whatever it's told.

Give it a vague idea and it fills the gaps with guesses. Teams spend weeks fixing work nobody asked for.

02

Plans get lost between departments.

Design sketches one thing, product writes another, engineering builds a third. Every handoff loses a little.

The fix

One shared plan, tested on paper first.

Sketch it, watch real people try it, then write the plan AI and engineering build from.
