What I speak on

Signature topics

Each talk flexes to a keynote, a fireside chat, or a hands-on workshop, and I tailor the examples to your audience. Here's what people walk away with.

01

Designing for AI Without the Hype

Where AI actually belongs in a product, and a framework your team can design against instead of bolting features onto a roadmap.

The audience leaves with
  • A way to decide where AI earns its place, and where it doesn't
  • Human-in-the-loop patterns that build trust instead of eroding it
  • How to spot and avoid "AI theater"
For: product & design teams, and the leaders who set their direction
02

Building Solo With AI

How AI coding agents let one person take a real product from concept to the App Store: what changes, what doesn't, and what it means for how teams work.

The audience leaves with
  • An honest picture of AI-native building, from someone who shipped it
  • Where craft, taste, and judgment still decide the outcome
  • How to raise their own ceiling without losing the plot
For: designers, founders, and product leaders
03

Calmer Products: Systems Thinking & Cognitive Load

Why reducing friction is the highest-leverage design skill, and how to build that discipline into a team instead of leaving it to heroics.

The audience leaves with
  • A lens for seeing cognitive load others miss
  • Patterns for taming dense, data-heavy products
  • How to make "make it simpler" a team habit, not a slogan
For: product & design teams working on complex software
04

Right-Sizing Design: Do You Even Need a Team Yet?

Most orgs over-invest in design too early or starve it too long. I help teams read their own design and research maturity, then pick the right level (from AI-assisted, to a fractional lead, to a full in-house function) and build it when they're actually ready.

The audience leaves with
  • A way to gauge their org's real design and research maturity
  • The right level for their stage: AI, fractional, contract, agency, or full team
  • A 0→1 playbook for when they're ready to build the function
For: founders, heads of design, and leaders deciding how much design to invest in

Short bio

Aneil Razvi is a product design and AI experience leader based in Dallas-Fort Worth. He helps companies decide where AI belongs in their products, and builds the design teams that ship them. He designed and shipped Cubby, his personal project, solo using AI-assisted tools.

Long bio

Aneil Razvi leads product design and AI experience. He built the design function at SPOTIO, where he set the AI experience strategy and helped shape DASH, an AI co-pilot for sales teams with human approval built in. He helped build the product design department at Southern New Hampshire University, unified Omnitracs's fleet platform, and led design work for Citi, Capital One and Kalkomey. His point of view: design should be sized to where a company actually is, and AI should earn its place in a product instead of being bolted on. He has spoken at Vista UX Summit and AIGA Dallas, teaches AI, Built On Paper, and designed and shipped Cubby, his personal project, solo using AI-assisted tools.

Before you book

What does Aneil Razvi speak about?

Human-centered AI product design: where AI belongs in a product and how to keep the trust of the people using it. He also speaks on building solo with AI-assisted tools, reducing cognitive load in complex products, and sizing design to a company's maturity. Each talk works as a keynote, a fireside chat or a hands-on workshop.

Is Aneil Razvi available for international events?

Yes. He is based in Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas, and travels for events worldwide. He has worked on-site with teams abroad, including in Poland, and he also speaks virtually.

Can the talk be tailored to our audience?

Yes. Every talk uses examples from your industry and is shaped around what your audience needs to do next.

Who are his talks for?

Product and design teams, the leaders who set their direction, founders deciding how much design to invest in, and design students.

Does he do podcasts and panels?

Yes. Recent appearances include Ramblings of a Designer, episode 177 (October 2026), and the AIGA Dallas Mini-Con industry panel at UT Dallas (October 2026).
