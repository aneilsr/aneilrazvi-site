What AI is actually doing to your job

Not a prediction. Not a vibe. Two public datasets, joined and cited: the tasks that make up your occupation, and how people are really using AI against each one. Type what you call yourself, not the official title.

55,283 job titles in the index, mapping to 1,016 occupations.

Sources and method

Occupations and tasks from the O*NET 31.0 database, US Department of Labor, CC BY 4.0. 1,016 occupations, 18,838 task statements, and the reported job titles people actually use. AI usage from the Anthropic Economic Index, 2026-06-26 release, CC BY. Global, task level and occupation level.

What the percentage is. Of the conversations where AI was used on this kind of work, the share that looked like automation, AI completing the task, rather than augmentation, a person working with AI. It is a description of how people use AI today. It is not a forecast, not a probability your job disappears, and not a measure of how much of the work AI can do.

Where it stops. 300 of the 1,016 occupations have no published figure. A blank in the source data means the value was not published, not that it is zero. Those occupations get no number here. Military occupations have no task statements in O*NET at all.
