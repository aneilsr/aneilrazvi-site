The course

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The course is built for people who don't. You think on paper, test with real people, and then give an AI tool a plan clear enough to build from.

How much time does it take?

Seven 90-minute Saturday sessions, plus a few hours of work between them. Every exercise has a timer, and when it ends you stop. That keeps you out of rabbit holes.

Will my whole app be finished?

Not every part, and that's on purpose. Your whole app will be designed on paper, tested with real people and planned. Then you build a first working version with an AI tool: real software, live at a web address anyone can open on their phone, with the home screen and your first part working. You leave knowing how to build every other part the same way.

Is the course already recorded?

The founding cohort is taught live, and each session becomes part of the self-paced course. You're not buying a video library. You're getting me, live, while it's being made.

Who owns what I make?

You do: your app, your paper and your documents. The course prompts and templates are for your own project.

The live cohort

Is it online or in person?

The founding cohort is online. "Live" means you join me in real time on a video call, Saturdays 10 to 11:30am Central, from wherever you are. It's not a pre-recorded video course. In-person workshops are a separate option for colleges and teams in Dallas-Fort Worth.

When does it run?

Seven Saturdays from Sat 31 Oct, 10 to 11:30am Central, with no session on Thanksgiving weekend. Session 7, on Sat 19 Dec, is demo day: you show your app. Enrollment closes Sat 24 Oct.

What if only one or two people join?

It still runs. A small group just means more of my time on your app.

What if I miss a session?

Every session is recorded and posted within a day, for the people in your cohort only. Recordings are never public or shared outside the group. You also get one 15-minute catch-up call with me, and if life gets in the way, you can move to a later cohort once, free.

Money and refunds

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund up to the start of session 2. After that, you can move to a later cohort instead.

Can I pay in two parts?

Yes. The founding cohort is $897, or two payments of $459.

What does the self-paced course cost?

$397, for steps 0 to 8 with every kit and template. It opens after the founding cohort, and founding members get it for life.

Is there an option for a school or team?

Yes. An in-person afternoon covers steps 1 to 5 with laptops closed. For colleges and student groups it's free this fall; for your own team or organization it's a private workshop. Book a 15-minute intro.

Tools and tech

Which AI tool do I need?

Claude or ChatGPT, on your own account. Your plan lives in a Project there from step 6 on. Step 7 helps you pick the tools your app runs on, and any accounts or costs for those are yours.

What else do I need?

Paper, pens and a phone to photograph your paper. For the live sessions, a computer with a camera for the video call. We also work on a shared FigJam board during sessions, and I'll send you the link.
