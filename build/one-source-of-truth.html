The path
Which AI does it work with?

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Any chat AI that can hold a conversation will do.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. The prompt is written for people who are new to this. You talk, it asks, and it writes.

How long does it take?

That depends on how much you have to say. You can stop and come back, and a ten-minute monthly check-up keeps the documents current.

Is it really free?

Yes, for your own business. Please don't resell or republish it.

What should I never share with the AI?

Passwords, bank details and customer names. The prompt tells the AI never to ask for them, and to leave them out if you share one by accident.
