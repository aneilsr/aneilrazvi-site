Why it matters

Anyone can make it look good now.

A clean screen used to prove you could make the thing. Now it takes thirty seconds and a sentence. So a beautiful portfolio doesn't prove what it used to.

What's left is judgment: what you chose, what you rejected, and where AI earns its place. That's what hiring managers are listening for when they ask how you use AI.

It's an honest retrospective. Your Then & Now is labeled as how you'd run the project today. You never claim you used AI back then. You show how you think now.

Questions

Do I need to know AI tools?

No. We talk through where AI would fit in plain language. You don't need to have used any tool to do this well.

Isn't this faking AI experience?

No. It's labeled as a retrospective: how you'd run the project today. You never claim you used AI back then. That honesty is the point, because it shows your judgment.

Which project should I pick?

We pick it together in the first session. Usually it's the one with the most decisions in it, not the prettiest one.
