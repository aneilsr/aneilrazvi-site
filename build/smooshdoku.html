Proof it works · Smooshdoku

Smooshdoku is going through the path right now.

A cozy logic puzzle: one friend in every row, column and color, and nobody touching. 200 levels across four seasons, free to play, no account. It's coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Android, and I'm launching it in the open so you can watch each part happen.

Join the playtesters Visit smooshdoku.com

The path, live

Where it is in each part.

This page follows the launch as it happens. Part 4 of the course is being written from it.

Part 1

Vision

Done
  • The world came first. Smoosh Valley is a shared world, with four places (Spring Meadow, Sunny Shore, Maple Hollow and Snowy Peak) and a cast of small friends. Every game lives inside it.
  • The rules are written down, short enough to learn in a minute: one per row and column, one per color, and no touching, not even at the corners.
  • What it will never be: loud or long. Small, cozy, and made to be played in ten calm minutes. No ads.
Part 2

Sketch & test

Open now
  • Playtesters are signing up on iPhone, iPad and Android. They're the "real people tested" step, before launch.
  • Every Smoosh Valley game gets playtesters, not just this one. The list carries over to the next game.

Want in? Join the playtesters. Your answers go to a Google Form and are used only to send test invites.

Part 3

Build with AI

Underway
  • Built the same way as Cubby: a written plan first, then AI tools, one part at a time, with every change checked before it's trusted.
  • 200 levels in four seasons of 50, each chapter ending with a crown level, then a gentle one.
Part 4

Launch

Next
  • Coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Android. Free to play, no account needed.
  • This page will track it: who it's for, where they are, the store rules, and what gets measured after launch.
  • Then the next Smoosh Valley game. With the world and its rules already written down, game two starts at Part 2.
