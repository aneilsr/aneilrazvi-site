The problem

AI made building fast. It didn't make deciding easier.

1

AI builds whatever it's told.

Give it a vague idea and it fills the gaps with guesses. The team spends weeks fixing work nobody asked for.

2

Plans get lost between departments.

Design sketches one thing, product writes another, engineering builds a third. Every handoff loses a little of what you meant.

3

The fix is one shared plan, tested on paper first.

Your team sketches it, watches real people try it, then writes the plan AI and engineering build from. Cheap to change, clear to everyone.

What your team leaves with

Something real, not a slide deck.

  • 1
    A tested designEvery screen on paper, tried by people who'd never seen it, and fixed where it broke.
  • 2
    The four plan documentsVision, rules, backlog and parking lot, in plain English, ready for AI tools and your engineers.
  • 3
    A first part builtOne piece working, built from the plan, so the team has seen the whole loop end to end.

The half-day workshop covers the tested design and gets the plan started. The virtual team track and Build cohort seats carry it through the four documents and a first part built.
