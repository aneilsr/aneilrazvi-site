AI, Built On Paper · the build prompt

Take your paper app into AI.

You drew your app on paper and watched real people try it. This prompt turns that paper into a plan, then a first clickable version. It works in any chat AI, like Claude or ChatGPT.

How to use it

How to use it

Four steps. Give it about an hour.

You can stop and come back. The chat keeps your place.

Step 1

Have your photos ready

Take one photo of each paper sheet, in order, starting with home.

Tip: lay each sheet flat, in good light, and fill the frame.

Step 2

Open a new chat

In Claude or ChatGPT, on your phone or a computer.

Tip: make a Project named after your app first, so your documents stay together.

Step 3

Paste the prompt and send it

Then answer its questions, one at a time. Attach your photos when it asks.

Step 4

Say "next" to move on

After the documents and the plan, it makes files for you. Save them.

The prompt

Copy it, paste it, send it.

It's long on purpose. Every line keeps the AI asking you instead of guessing.

The build prompt · 5 stages
[Source: the AI, Built On Paper method by Aneil Razvi (aneilrazvi.com). For your own project. Please don't resell or republish.]

I drew an app on paper and tested it with real people. Be my planning partner. Help me turn it into a plan, then a first clickable version. We go one stage at a time. At the end of each stage, tell me to say "next" when I'm ready. Start Stage 1 now.

Stage 1. Interview me about my idea.
Ask one question at a time, about 8 in all. Find out:
- my app's name (it can be "no name yet")
- who it's for, and what is hard for them today
- the one job my app does for them
- how I'll know it worked
- what my app will never do
- where people will use it: phone, tablet or computer
- what I learned when someone tested my paper
Then say my idea back in one sentence and ask if it's right.

Stage 2. Look at my paper.
I'll attach photos. Each sheet is one part of my app. For each photo, tell me in one line what you see. Ask about anything you can't read. If a part isn't drawn, ask what I expect there. Don't invent it. No photos? Ask me to describe each screen.

Stage 3. Write my four documents.
One at a time. Show each one, then wait for my "ok" or my changes. Use my words. Keep each to one page.
- Vision: my idea in one sentence, who it's for, the one job, what done looks like, what it will never do.
- Rules: how every screen behaves. Same symbols, same buttons, same words. What must always or never happen.
- Backlog: every part of my app, each with a "done when" check I can test by hand.
- Parking lot: ideas I mentioned that aren't in this version.
When all four are approved, make each one its own file I can download and edit. A Word file is best. Name each with my app's name and the document, like "Pantry Pal - Vision". If you can't make files, put each one in its own copy box with its title on top. Always put Rules in a copy box too.
Then tell me where to keep them. In a Project, add Vision, Backlog and Parking lot to the files, and paste Rules into the instructions. No Project? Save all four in a folder named after my app.

Stage 4. Write my project plan.
One page. Put the parts in build order: home first, then the part that proves my idea works. For each step, say what we build and the "done when" check I'll test by hand. End with the loop after every step: test it, fix it, update the right document. When I approve it, make it a file the same way, like "Pantry Pal - Plan".

Stage 5. Build it.
Only when I say "build it." Make a clickable version that looks like my paper, in black and white. Every button goes somewhere. Parts that aren't built yet show "Not built yet." If you can't show a preview here, say so and give me the steps for the first part instead.

Rules for you:
- It's my app. Don't add parts I didn't ask for.
- Short, plain sentences. One question at a time.
- If a question is hard, give me 2 or 3 choices to pick from.
- If you're not sure, ask. Don't guess.
- No code before Stage 5.
Paste it into a new chat

What happens

Five stages, one at a time.

The AI waits for you to say "next" before it moves on. Nothing gets built until you say "build it."

Stage 1

The interview

About 8 questions about your idea, one at a time: who it's for, the one job, and what you learned from testing.

You get: your idea, said back in one sentence.

Stage 2

Your paper

It reads each photo back to you in one line, and asks about anything it can't read or that isn't drawn yet.

You get: an AI that knows your app, with nothing made up.

Stage 3

Four documents

Vision, Rules, Backlog and Parking lot, one page each, in your words. You approve each one.

You get: four files you can download and edit.

Stage 4

The project plan

Your parts in build order: home first, then the part that proves your idea works. Each step has a check you test by hand.

You get: a one-page plan file.

Stage 5

Build it

A clickable version that looks like your paper, in black and white. Parts that aren't built yet say so.

You get: your app on a screen.

Where your documents go

Project files

  • Vision
  • Backlog
  • Parking lot
  • Your project plan

Project instructions

  • Rules, pasted in, so every chat in the Project follows them

Keep them apart. When something breaks later, you need to know whether it broke your vision, a rule, or just one part. No Project? Save them in a folder named after your app.

Words to use next

Type these into the same chat.

  • nextYou're ready for the next stage.
  • build itYour plan is done and you want the clickable version.
  • Change only this: ___. Keep everything else the same.One thing is wrong.
  • Someone got stuck on ___. Help me fix that screen.After you test it with someone.
  • New idea: ___. Put it in the Parking lot.A new idea comes up while you build.
  • It did something I didn't expect: ___. Which of my documents was wrong? Fix that one first.The AI surprises you.
  • Let's build the next part: ___. Here are my four documents and my plan.You start a fresh chat for each part.
