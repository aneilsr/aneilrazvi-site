When it makes sense, and when it doesn't

I'd rather lose the work than sell you the wrong shape of it.

A good fit

  • You're adding AI to a product and nobody owns how it should behave for the person using it.
  • You have designers, but no senior design leader setting direction.
  • You need design decisions made this quarter, before a full-time executive makes sense.
  • You've merged products or platforms, and the experience has split into pieces.

Not a fit

  • You need someone five days a week, every week. Hire full-time; I'll help you shape the role.
  • You don't need design leadership yet. The free Design Maturity Assessment will tell you, and so will I.

Why AI products need a design lead

Most AI features fail on trust, not on the model. People stop using them when they can't tell what the system did, why it did it, or how to correct it. Deciding where AI belongs, where a person stays in the loop, and what the product says when it's unsure are design decisions. Someone senior has to own them.

At SPOTIO I set the AI experience strategy and helped shape DASH, an AI co-pilot for sales teams with human approval built in. At Omnitracs I designed an ML next-best-action framework into a fleet platform used by fleets running about one million vehicles. The pattern is the same: AI suggests, people decide, and the product makes that easy.

Questions

What does a fractional head of design do?

A fractional head of design owns design decisions for part of the week: direction, critique, standards, hiring input, and hands-on work on the most important problems. For an AI product, Aneil Razvi also decides where AI belongs and how it should behave for the person using it.

How many days a week does a fractional head of design work?

With Aneil Razvi, usually two days a week, on the same days every week. Some engagements start smaller, as a one-week First Look or a fixed-scope sprint.

Does Aneil Razvi work with companies outside the US?

Yes. He works remotely with teams anywhere and travels for on-site work when it helps. He has worked on-site with teams abroad, including in Poland.

How much does a fractional head of design cost?

My prices are published up front on Work With Me, including the monthly fractional retainer and the fixed-price sprints.

Can a fractional head of design lead an existing design team?

Yes. Aneil coaches the team, sets career ladders and rituals, gives hiring input, and raises the bar on craft and process while staying hands-on.

What if we don't need a head of design yet?

Then I'll say so. The free Design Maturity Assessment takes about four minutes and points to the right level for your stage: AI-assisted, fractional, contractors, an agency, or a full team.

How fast can a fractional head of design start?

Usually within days: a short call, then you pick the shape of the engagement together, and he is embedded with your team from week one.
