Let's Talk

For anyone carrying an idea

You have an idea. Here is how to find out where you actually are with it.

Most advice about ideas is someone guessing, usually confidently. This is not that. Below is a prompt you paste into Claude or ChatGPT. It walks through your idea with you and tells you three things: what you have actually decided, what you are assuming, and what nobody can know yet. That last one matters most, and it is the part everyone skips.

Free · no email needed

The idea map

Copy this, paste it into whichever assistant you use, and answer its questions honestly. It will not design anything until it has asked you what it needs to know. That is deliberate.

Prompt


    
That box scrolls. The whole thing is about forty lines and you do not have to read it here. Copy it and let it ask you the questions.

    

      
      
    

    

  


  

    
What it does

    
    
      
  • 1Asks you to say the idea in plain words, then asks who it is for. Not a market. A person.
    • 
      
  • 2Separates what you know from what you believe. Most ideas die because nobody made that list.
    • 
      
  • 3Names the smallest version that would prove or kill the idea, and what that version would cost in time rather than money.
    • 
      
  • 4Marks what cannot be known yet, and says so plainly instead of filling it with a confident guess.
    • 
      
  • 5Gives you one thing to do this week. One.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Why the unknowns get their own section. A plan that hides its guesses feels better and is worth less. If you can see which three assumptions the whole thing rests on, you can go test those three instead of building for six months and finding out.

Talk it through with me

      
A few of these a month

      
Twenty minutes, no pitch, no deck. You describe the idea and I tell you what I would do next and what I would not bother with. If it turns out you need something built, we can talk about that separately. Most people do not.
