AI will build whatever you describe. Including the wrong thing.

Building used to be slow, and the slowness caught bad ideas before they cost much. That safety net is gone. This course puts it back where it's cheapest: on paper, in front of real people, before anything gets built.

Is this for you?

Yes, if

  • You have an idea you keep coming back to
  • You've never written code, or don't want to
  • You'd rather test it on people than guess
  • You can give it Saturday mornings and a few evenings

Skip it if

  • You want someone to build it for you
  • You want to learn to code
  • You're looking for a get-rich-quick app

How it works

Ten steps in four stages. Each stage ends with something you can hold.

Stage 1Steps 0 to 2

Define

Your idea in one sentence, what done looks like, and everything it could do, sorted on one page.

You leave with: the whole app on one page
Stage 2Step 3

Paper

Every part of your app on paper, one sheet per part, starting where your users start.

You leave with: the product, before spending a dollar
Stage 3Steps 4 and 5

Test

Hand it to people who have never seen it, watch where they get lost, and fix the paper.

You leave with: a tested design, in photos
Stage 4Steps 6 to 9

Build and ship

Turn the paper into a plan an AI can follow, build it one part at a time, and set up how it takes money.

You leave with: a first version of your app, live

    Questions people ask first

    Is it online or in person?

    The founding cohort is online. "Live" means you join me in real time on a video call, Saturdays 10 to 11:30am Central, from wherever you are. It's not a pre-recorded video course. In-person workshops are a separate option for colleges and teams in Dallas-Fort Worth.

    Do I need to know how to code?

    No. The course is built for people who don't. You think on paper, test with real people, and then give an AI tool a plan clear enough to build from.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes, a full refund up to the start of session 2. After that, you can move to a later cohort instead.
