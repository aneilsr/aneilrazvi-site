The ten steps

Steps, not weeks. Every exercise has a timer, and you move at your own pace.

On paper

  • 0Before you start: your idea in one sentence.
  • 1Outcome first: who it is for, and the one job it does.
  • 2Mind dump: everything it could do, sorted into areas and parts.
  • 3Paper the whole app, starting at sign-up or the home screen. One sheet per part.
  • 4Break it, twice: hand it to someone, give them a task, and watch. Where something isn't drawn, ask what they expected to see.
  • 5Change it: fix the paper from what you saw, and photograph every sheet.

In order

  • 6Set up a project in Claude or ChatGPT, then translate: our prompt, your photos and its questions turn into four documents.
  • 7Put the parts in build order and pick your tools.

To ship

  • 8Build it: the whole app clickable first, then one part at a time made real.
  • 9Business basics, and a named expert for the specifics.

You follow one example the whole way. At every step you see the same recipe app at that same stage: the mind dump, the paper, the test, the documents, the live app. You build your own app; the recipe app shows you what done looks like.

Questions

    
Do I need to know how to code?
No. The course is built for people who don't. You think on paper, test with real people, and then give an AI tool a plan clear enough to build from.

    
How much time does it take?
It depends on you, which is why there are steps instead of weeks. Every exercise has a timer, and when it ends you stop. The founding cohort meets for seven 90-minute Saturday sessions, with work in between.

    
What if I miss a live session?
Every session is recorded and posted within a day, and you get one short catch-up call with me. If life gets in the way, you can move to a later cohort once, free.

    
Will my whole app be finished?
Your whole app will be on paper, tested and planned, and clickable at a real address, with home and your first part working. You leave knowing how to build every other part the same way, one at a time.

    
Who owns what I make?
You do: your app, your paper and your documents. The course prompts and templates are for your own project.
