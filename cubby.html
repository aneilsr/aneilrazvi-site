Cubby · Caregiver-Curated Video for Autism & IDD

A caregiver-curated video app, designed and built solo

Founder and Builder · 2026–Present · cubbyplaylist.com

Cubby is a caregiver-curated video app for teens and adults with autism and intellectual and developmental disabilities. A trusted adult builds a locked-down library of approved videos; the viewer watches only what's chosen: no search, no recommendations, no autoplay. I designed, built, and shipped it solo, end to end, using AI-native tools, live on the App Store.

The Context

An underserved community, a calmer way to watch

I built Cubby for kids first, then the caregivers I interviewed redirected me. Teens and adults with autism and IDD had no calm, locked-down way to watch, and the people who support them were stuck stitching together workarounds.

So I pointed Cubby at them. Caregiver interviews confirmed the need, and the pivot walked me out of the COPPA minefield before I committed the real build.

What I Did

Concept to App Store, owned end to end

  • Designed, built, and shipped Cubby solo, 0 to 1 (concept through design, engineering, compliance, and launch) as a live iOS App Store release and production web app
  • Repositioned from a saturated kids-video market to the underserved autism and IDD segment after competitive and legal analysis, removing COPPA exposure and validating with caregiver interviews
  • Built an accessibility-first, sensory-adaptive design system: per-profile calm, balanced, and engage modes, with the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface
  • Designed a three-role dignity model (Admin, Curator, Viewer) so a trusted adult curates and the viewer watches only what's chosen: no search, recommendations, or autoplay
  • Built on React and TypeScript (Capacitor for iOS, Supabase, Stripe, Anthropic API), using AI coding agents daily

The Turn

Where it started, where it is now

The strongest decision on Cubby was a pivot: away from a crowded kids-video market, toward a community that actually needed what I was building.

Where it started

A kids-video concept in a crowded market

Cubby began as a parent-controlled kids' viewing app: a Parent Hub, child profiles, and a curated feed of kid-safe videos. The space was saturated, COPPA-heavy, and hard to differentiate.

Where it is now

A caregiver-curated app for autism and IDD

After competitive and legal analysis and caregiver interviews, I repositioned to an underserved community: teens and adults with autism and IDD. The same curated-video core, rebuilt around a Viewer experience and accessibility controls (contrast, dim, and text size) that this audience actually needs.

Impact

Proof, not a prototype

  • A working, shippable iOS app, live on the App Store at cubbyplaylist.com
  • A repositioning that turned a crowded-market product into one an underserved community needs
  • Proof of an AI-fluent design leader who still ships, concept to product
