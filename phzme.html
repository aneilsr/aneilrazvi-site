The Context

Vision into an experience investors could feel

An early-stage startup needed brand, product experience, and an investor-ready story, built quickly, working directly with the founder.

  • Led fundraising pitch development and investor storytelling, translating the vision into compelling narratives
  • Drove experience strategy across the full customer journey, building the brand promise into the product
