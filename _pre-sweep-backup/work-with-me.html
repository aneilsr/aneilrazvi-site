Work With Me

Senior design leadership, and the hands to back it up

I partner with companies that need senior design leadership and the hands to back it up. Sometimes that's a full-time executive role. Sometimes it's a fractional engagement where you get the strategy, the team-building, and the craft without the full-time hire. Either way I sit on the same side of the table as you and your team, focused on what we can actually ship.

What I can do for you

Fractional Head or VP of Design, or CXO

Set the design direction, build and coach the team, and raise the bar on craft and process.

AI experience strategy

Define where AI genuinely belongs in your product, and a framework your team can design against, instead of bolting on features.

0-to-1 and R&D

Take a concept from someone's head to a working, shippable product with a lean team and a fast clock.

Design org and operations

Stand up the function, set the career ladders and rituals, and build the research and design-ops backbone that lets it scale.

Brand and experience direction

Identity, motion, and the through-line that makes a product feel like one thing.

Design maturity assessment

Figure out whether you're ready for design and research, and at what level: AI-assisted, fractional, contract, agency, or a full team. A clear read, not a pitch for a big hire.

Start here if you're not sure

How much design do you actually need?

Most companies over-invest in design too early or starve it for too long. Before you hire anyone, I'll read where your org actually is and point you to the right level of investment for your stage.

Level 01

AI-assisted

The right tools, no dedicated designers. Enough to ship and learn while design isn't the bottleneck yet.

Level 02

Fractional lead

One senior designer, part-time. Direction and craft without a full-time hire. Often where I come in.

Level 03

Independent contractors

A few specialists for the moment. Flexible hands when the work comes in bursts.

Level 04

External firm

An agency for a big, bounded push. Capacity and polish, on a clock.

Level 05

In-house team

A built-out design org. The right call once design is core to how you compete.

I'll tell you which rung you're actually on, even when the honest answer is "you don't need me yet." That's how you end up trusting the rest of the advice.
